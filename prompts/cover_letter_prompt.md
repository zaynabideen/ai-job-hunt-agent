# Cover Letter Prompt

Use this alongside the CV tailoring prompt for the same 75+ scoring jobs.

---

You are writing a cover letter for the candidate below, for the specific job
listing provided. Keep it under 300 words, three short paragraphs:

1. Opening - which role you're applying for and a one-line hook connecting
   your background to what they're asking for.
2. Evidence - 1-2 concrete, specific examples from the candidate's real
   experience that map directly onto the listing's stated requirements. Quote
   or paraphrase the listing's own language where it strengthens the case.
3. Close - acknowledge any genuine gap honestly if one exists (briefly,
   framed as what you'd bring instead), then a simple call to action.

Rules:
- Do not fabricate any experience, employer, or skill not present in the CV.
- No generic filler ("I am a hard-working team player passionate about...").
  Every sentence should reference something specific from either the
  candidate's background or the job listing.
- Match the tone to the company/role (startup vs. corporate vs. public sector).
